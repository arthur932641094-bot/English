Phrasal Verbs & Chunks

动词短语 · 搭配1 / 7

start off

chunk

一件事最开头的那一下，或者"一开始是……的状态"。

To begin something, or to begin as / at a certain point.

  • We started off with a quick meeting.
  • He started off as an intern, now he runs the team.

send off

chunk

把东西寄出去、发出去，人交给邮政/系统之后就不在你手上了。

To post or dispatch something so it's on its way.

  • I sent off the documents yesterday.
  • Have you sent off the application yet?

set off / take off

chunk

出发、启程。set off 是人上路；take off 是飞机离地。

Set off = to start a journey. Take off = a plane leaves the ground.

  • We set off at 7am to beat the traffic.
  • The plane took off on time.

pay off

chunk

两个意思：把钱还清；付出终于有了回报。

To clear a debt in full, or for effort to finally bring a reward.

  • All the hard work finally paid off.
  • It took years, but we paid off the mortgage.

slack off

chunk

偷懒摸鱼，该干的活松了劲。

To be lazy and stop putting in effort, especially at work.

  • I've been slacking off at work all day.
  • The moment the boss leaves, everyone slacks off.

get dumped

chunk

被甩了，对方主动结束的那种。

To be broken up with by your partner.

  • He dumped me over text — so cold.
  • She got dumped a week before their anniversary.

be over being + adj/n

chunk

受够了这个状态，不想再这样下去了。

To be sick of a situation and done putting up with it.

  • I'm over being single. 受够单身了
  • She's over being treated like a child.
  • I'm over working overtime all the time.

同义： I'm done with… / I'm sick of…。后面接 being + adj/n，或 doing。

catch up on

chunk

把落下的进度补回来。

To do work you fell behind on and get current again.

  • I stayed late to catch up on work.
  • I need to catch up on sleep this weekend.

自然搭配：catch up on work / sleep / emails / the news。别说 "catch up on the progress"（不地道）。

bump into

chunk

偶遇——没约就撞见了熟人。

To meet someone by chance, without planning it.

  • I bumped into my old teacher at the supermarket.
  • Funny bumping into you here!

近义区别：run into 最万能；bump into 更口语随意；come across 偏"偶然发现某物/信息"，不只对人 —— He came across an old photo while cleaning.

pop into

chunk

顺道、快速进去一下，待不久那种；也说念头"突然冒出来"（pop into my head）。

To go into a place quickly and briefly — or for a thought to suddenly come to mind.

  • I'll just pop into the shop for some milk. 顺道进去一下
  • Can you pop into my office for a sec? 进来一下
  • A weird idea popped into my head. 突然冒出个念头

同家族：pop in 顺道来一下 · pop out 出去一会儿 · pop over / round 过来串个门。

blank out

chunk

脑子突然一片空白、想不起来（考试/紧张时）；也指把内容"涂掉、遮盖"。

To suddenly forget / go blank; or to cover or erase text.

  • I completely blanked out in the interview. 面试脑子一片空白
  • My mind just blanked out. 大脑当机、想不起来
  • They blanked out the names in the document. 把名字涂掉

近义 go blank：My mind went blank. 脑子一片空白。

rub against (each other)

chunk

相互摩擦、互相蹭——两个面接触着来回蹭（机械零件、皮肤、树枝）。

For two surfaces to move against each other with friction.

  • The gears rub against each other. 齿轮相互摩擦
  • My shoes rub against my heels. 鞋磨脚后跟
  • Branches rubbing against the window. 树枝蹭着窗户

rub 家族：rub off（蹭掉；也"影响到别人" his calm rubbed off on me）· rub in（don't rub it in = 别哪壶不开提哪壶）· rub out（擦掉）。

put someone behind

chunk

让某人（进度）落后、耽误了某人——尤指时间/计划上被拖慢。

To make someone fall behind on schedule or progress.

  • The delay put me behind. 那次延误耽误了我
  • Getting sick really put me behind on work. 生病让我工作严重落后
  • Now we're behind schedule. 落后于计划了

别混：put something behind you = 把某事翻篇、抛诸脑后（put the past behind you）；fall/be behind = 落后。

be after (something)

chunk

"你要的是什么 / 你想要啥"——be after sth = 想要、追求、找某样东西（口语）。

To want, seek, or be looking for something.

  • What are you after? 你想要什么 / 你找什么
  • Is this what you're after? 这是你要的吗
  • He's after a promotion. 他想升职
  • The police are after him. 警察在追捕他

be after = want / seek（口语）；也指"追捕、追着要"。

pick up

chunk

超高频短语，一堆意思：捡起 / 接人 / 学会 / 接电话 / 好转 / 顺便买 / 搭讪勾搭 / 闻到侦测。

A very common phrasal verb with lots of senses.

  • pick up the pen 捡起
  • I'll pick you up at 7. 七点去接你
  • pick up some Spanish 顺便学点西语
  • Business is picking up. 生意好转
  • pick up some milk 顺便买点牛奶
  • He tried to pick her up at the bar. 在酒吧搭讪她
  • pick up a signal / scent 接收信号 / 闻到气味

pick-up line = 搭讪台词；pick someone up 才是"搭讪/勾搭"义，别和"接人"混——看语境。

go out

chunk

几个常用意思：熄灭/断电 · 外出 · 约会交往 · 过时 · 发出播出 · 退潮。

A common phrasal verb with several senses.

  • The lights went out. 灯灭了 / 停电了
  • go out for dinner 出去吃饭
  • They've been going out for a year. 他俩交往一年了
  • That style went out years ago. 那风格早过时了
  • The email went out this morning. 邮件早上发出去了
  • The tide goes out. 退潮

"突然坏了/关掉"就是熄灭/断电那义（灯、火、电、信号 go out）。

sign off (on) / sign it off

chunk

签字批准、拍板确认通过；sign off 也指（说完话/广播）收尾、下线。

To formally approve/authorise; or to end a message/broadcast.

  • The boss signed off on the budget. 老板批准了预算
  • Can you sign off on this? 你能签字确认吗
  • I need someone to sign it off. 得有人签字批准
  • I'll sign off now — talk soon. （结尾）我先撤了，回聊

sign off on sth = 批准/拍板；别和 sign up（报名）、sign in（签到登录）混。

looking back / look back on

chunk

"现在回想起来……"——常放句首，引出对过去的感慨；look back on 是回顾/回味一段经历。也可字面"回头看"。

"Looking back, …" = now that I reflect on it; look back on = to reflect on the past.

  • Looking back, I should've taken that job. 现在回想起来
  • She looks back on her uni years fondly. 回味大学时光
  • He looked back to check the door was locked. 字面：回头看

lead up to / the lead-up

chunk

leading up to 某事 = 通往、临近那件事的那段时间里（越来越近，那件事是时间终点）；也有"一步步铺垫、引向"的意思。关键：那件事只是参照点，不一定是你造成的。

In the run-up to an event (X is the reference point); or to gradually build toward it — not always cause it.

  • I had diarrhoea the whole week leading up to the wedding. 婚礼前那一整周
  • In the lead-up to the election, tensions rose. 大选前那阵子
  • the events leading up to the war 一步步导致战争的那些事（这句才带因果）

名词形式写作 the lead-up（带连字符）。diarrhoea 不可数，别加 the。

put up with

chunk

忍受、容忍——不喜欢但只能受着，没办法。

To tolerate something annoying without complaining or leaving.

  • I can't put up with the noise anymore. 受不了这噪音了
  • She put up with his temper for years. 忍了他的脾气好多年
  • How do you put up with him? 你怎么受得了他

≈ tolerate / stand，但更口语。

go on / where it goes

chunk

东西"套上去 / 放在……上面 / 属于哪里"。问东西该放哪也用它。

Where something is placed, fitted onto, or belongs.

  • Does this jacket go on over the hoodie?
  • Does this sauce go on the chips or the burger?
  • Where does this go? / Does this go here?

近义家族：be placed on / listed on / included on / belong on。

flip (family)

chunk

核心是"翻/突然一下"。翻书、突然发火、翻个面、拨开关、竖中指。

A quick flip or turn — of pages, of mood, of a switch.

  • I was flipping through a magazine. 随手翻
  • He flipped / flipped out when he heard the news. 突然炸了
  • On the flip side, it saves us money. 换个角度
  • Flip the switch. / He flipped me off 🖕

go through

chunk

熬过一段苦日子、经历一堆事。

To live through a hard or difficult experience.

  • He went through a lot when he first moved to Australia.
  • She's going through a rough patch right now.

go over

chunk

从头到尾仔细过一遍——检查、复习、或一起讨论。

To carefully review or run through something together.

  • Let's go over the plan one more time before the meeting.
  • Can you go over my essay and check for mistakes?

come down with

chunk

开始生病了，感觉要感冒/发烧那种苗头。

To start catching an illness, usually a mild one.

  • I think I've come down with a cold.
  • Half the office has come down with the flu.

come across as

chunk

给人一种……的印象（不一定是真的，是别人看你的感觉）。

To give people a certain impression of what you're like.

  • He comes across as arrogant, but he's actually shy.
  • Be careful — that email comes across as rude.

take over / get over

chunk

take over = 接手、接管；get over = 从（打击/病/感情）里走出来。

Take over = take control of. Get over = recover from something.

  • Can you take over for me while I'm on leave?
  • It took him months to get over the breakup.

roll over

chunk

没用完的东西自动结转到下个周期。

For something unused to carry over to the next period.

  • The unused data rolls over to next month.
  • Do my leave days roll over if I don't use them?

top up

chunk

加满、充值——把少了的补上去。

To add more to bring something back up to full.

  • Let me top up your glass.
  • I need to top up my phone / transport card.

draw lots

chunk

抽签决定。

To decide something randomly by drawing lots.

  • We couldn't decide, so we drew lots.

catch someone off guard

chunk

让人措手不及，完全没防备的时候被击中。

To surprise someone when they're completely unprepared.

  • The question really caught me off guard.
  • Her sudden resignation caught everyone off guard.

let's bounce

chunk

走吧、撤了，很随意的口语。

Casual slang for "let's leave / let's get out of here."

  • This party's dead — let's bounce.

have been + doing / adj

grammar

从过去某点一直持续到现在的状态或动作。

A state or action that started earlier and is still going now.

  • My foot has been numb since last night.
  • I have been feeling off all week.

stats padding

chunk

刷数据、堆数据——为了让自己的成绩单/统计数字好看，故意去做一些没什么实际价值、但能拉高数字的事情。体育里常见（比赛快结束了故意投几个空位球拉篮板数），职场里也能用，比如为了 KPI 好看去干一些没意义的活。带贬义，说人"注水"。

Doing low-value actions just to inflate your statistics/numbers, not because they actually matter.

  • Critics say he's just stats padding in garbage time. 批评者说他只是在垃圾时间里刷数据。
  • Don't waste time on tasks that are just stats padding for your resume. 别把时间浪费在那些只是为了简历数据好看的事情上。
  • Her assists this season look inflated — some call it stat padding. 她这赛季的助攻数看着有点虚，有人说这是在刷数据。

hanging down

chunk

垂下来、耷拉着——东西因为重力自然往下垂，头发、树枝、胳膊没力气的时候都能这么说，就是很日常的一个搭配。

Drooping or dangling downward due to gravity.

  • Her hair was hanging down over her shoulders. 她的头发披散在肩膀上。
  • The branches were hanging down so low they touched the ground. 树枝垂得很低，都碰到地面了。
  • He stood there with his arms hanging down limply. 他站在那儿，胳膊无力地垂着。

catch up with

chunk

追上、赶上——原本落后了（人、进度、消息），现在追上去了；也常用来指"和好久没见的人聚一聚，聊聊近况"；还有一层意思是"（做过的坏事）最终报应到你头上了"。

To reach the same level/position as someone/something; to meet up and share news; (fig.) consequences finally reaching you.

  • I ran faster to catch up with the group. 我加快脚步追上了大家。
  • Let's catch up with each other over coffee sometime. 找时间喝咖啡聊聊近况吧。
  • His lies finally caught up with him. 他的谎言最终还是让他付出了代价。

catch up

chunk

不带介词单独用，两个常见意思：①"叙叙旧、聊聊近况"，好久没见坐下来聊聊彼此发生了什么；②"赶上进度、补回落下的东西"，比如工作、学习落后了，现在追上来了。

To talk and share news after being apart; to reach the same level/progress after falling behind.

  • We should grab lunch and catch up. 我们该一起吃个午饭叙叙旧。
  • I fell behind on the reading, but I'm catching up now. 我阅读进度落后了，不过现在在追上来。
  • He missed a week of school and had a lot to catch up on. 他缺了一周课，有很多要补的。

常和 on 连用（catch up on...），跟 catch up on 卡片是同一族搭配。

cling wrap

chunk

保鲜膜，包食物用的那种透明塑料膜（美式常说 plastic wrap / Saran wrap）。

Thin transparent plastic film used to cover food.

  • Cover the bowl with cling wrap and put it in the fridge. 用保鲜膜盖住碗放进冰箱。
  • Wrap the sandwich in cling wrap. 把三明治用保鲜膜包起来。

resealable bag

chunk

可反复封口的袋子，就是自封袋/密封袋（比如 Ziploc 那种拉链袋），用完能再封上，不是一次性撕开就废了的那种。

A bag that can be sealed and opened again multiple times.

  • Store the leftovers in a resealable bag. 把剩菜放进自封袋里保存。
  • These snacks come in a resealable bag so they stay fresh. 这些零食是自封袋装的，能保持新鲜。

cynical /ˈsɪnɪkl/ adj.

word

啥都不信，谁做啥都觉得背后有私心。

Distrusting people's motives, expecting selfishness behind anything.

  • I'm a bit cynical about online influencers.
  • "You think he'll actually help?" — "No. Call me cynical."

A cynical person assumes the worst motive, not the worst outcome. 反面：He always gives people the benefit of the doubt.（总愿意先往好处想）

compatible (with) /kəmˈpætəbl/ adj.

word

合得来、能配套，两样东西/两个人不打架。

  • This charger isn't compatible with my phone.
  • They're just not compatible — always arguing.

harsh /hɑːʃ/ adj.

word

刺——话难听、态度苛刻、光线/声音扎人。

assimilate /əˈsɪmɪleɪt/ v.

word

To blend into a group/culture; or to absorb and take in (information, food).

①融入（into a group）；②吸收/消化（information / nutrients）。名词 assimilation。

Grammar & Usage

语法 · 辨析5 / 7

few / fewer vs little / less

grammar

能数的用 few/fewer（人、朋友、选项）；不能数的用 little/less（钱、时间、水）。

Few/fewer for countable things; little/less for uncountable.

  • Few people · fewer options left （可数）
  • Less money · less time left （不可数）

加 "a" 就是"没那么少、还有一点"：a few friends（有几个）· a little money（有点钱）。不加 a 则强调"很少"。

left vs rest（剩下）

grammar

rest = 一个整体里"其余那部分"；left = 发生了什么之后"还剩着的"。

Rest = the other part of a whole. Left = what remains after something happened.

  • I'll finish the rest of the chicken. 本来有的里剩下的
  • Two days left · three tickets left · no money left

left 常放名词后面（two days left）；rest 常用 the rest / the rest of + n。

rarely vs barely

grammar

rarely 说的是"很少发生"（频率）；barely 说的是"几乎没有 / 勉强"（程度）。

Rarely = frequency (seldom). Barely = degree (only just).

  • I rarely eat out. 很少出去吃
  • I barely passed the exam. 勉强及格

grammar

"最晚周五之前"——截止到那个点，不能更晚。

No later than Friday — the deadline is that point.

grammar

Ways to state how often something happens.

  • Every 2 years 每两年一次
  • Twice every three years 三年里两次

grammar

Both forms are fine and mean the same thing.

past vs passed

grammar

俩词同音，写的时候老搞混。passed 只是动词 pass 的过去式；past 是"过去/经过"的名词、介词、形容词——永远不当动词。

passed = past tense of the verb "pass". past = everything else (noun / prep / adj), never a verb.

  • I passed the exam. 动词：通过
  • She passed me the salt. 动词：递
  • I walked past the shop. 介词：经过
  • Let's forget the past. 名词：过去
  • It's half past three. 介词：…点过

判断法：是"做了 pass 这个动作"（通过 / 经过 / 递 / 流逝）→ passed；其它一律 past。

the + 比较级…, the + 比较级…（越…越…）

grammar

"越……越……"——两样东西同步变化。结构：The + 比较级 …, the + 比较级 …

"The …-er …, the …-er …" — two things changing together.

  • The higher the temperature, the more powerful the AC. 温度越高，空调越猛
  • The more you practise, the better you get. 练得越多越熟
  • The sooner, the better. 越快越好

两半都用比较级；口语可极简：The bigger the better / The sooner the better。

变弱 / 衰减 · getting weaker

cluster

一堆"变弱 / 衰减"的近义词，按最典型的用词场景分清楚，别混用。

A map of "get weaker / decline" verbs, sorted by their typical scenario.

weaken · 泛指变弱

The signal weakened as we drove into the tunnel. 进隧道信号变弱

fade · 声音、光、信号慢慢消失

The music slowly faded out. 音乐渐弱消失

attenuate · 信号、电磁波、振动（工程衰减）

The cable attenuates the signal over distance. 信号随距离衰减

decay · 幅值、能量、信号随时间衰减

The vibration amplitude decays over time. 振幅随时间衰减

degrade · 性能、材料、系统退化

Performance degrades under heavy load. 高负载下性能退化

deteriorate · 状况、质量、健康恶化

His health deteriorated quickly. 健康迅速恶化

decline · 数据、能力、健康下降

Sales have declined this year. 今年销量下降

drop off · 数值或强度明显下降

Attendance dropped off after week one. 第一周后出席明显下降

tail off · 后半段逐渐减弱

His enthusiasm tailed off. 热情渐渐减退

die down / die away · 声音、震动、风等自然消退

The wind died down by evening. 傍晚风渐渐平息

工程/物理衰减多用 attenuate / decay；恶化用 deteriorate / degrade；自然消退用 die down / fade / tail off。

"说明 / 解释" · explain 家族

cluster

中文一个"说明"，英文一大把，看你想表达哪种"说明"。

Many English verbs map to "说明" — pick by exactly what kind of explaining you mean.

explain · 最通用："解释清楚"

Can you explain how this works? 你能说明一下这个怎么运作吗

describe · 描述具体情况（what it's like，不是 why）

Please describe the issue. 说明一下这个 issue 的具体情况

illustrate · 用例子/图表/数据来说明

This chart illustrates the trend clearly. 这图清楚地说明了趋势

demonstrate · 展示并证明（比 illustrate 强；process / ability / evidence）

The test results demonstrate that the device is stable. 测试结果证明设备稳定

clarify · 澄清，把模糊的地方说明白

Could you clarify what you mean? 你能澄清一下你的意思吗

indicate · 表明、显示（data / sign / result）

The results indicate a possible fault. 结果表明可能有故障

show · 最口语自然，很多时候能代替"说明"

This shows that the cable is disconnected. 这说明线缆断开了

specify · 明确说明、具体规定

Please specify the required voltage. 请明确规定所需电压

state · 陈述、明确写明（偏正式）

The report states that no damage was found. 报告写明未发现损坏

速记：解释原理 explain · 描述情况 describe · 举例说明 illustrate · 展示证明 demonstrate · 澄清 clarify · 表明 indicate · 口语 show · 明确规定 specify · 正式陈述 state。
