Phrasal Verbs & Chunks

动词短语 · 搭配1 / 7

start off

chunk

一件事最开头的那一下，或者"一开始是……的状态"。

To begin something, or to begin as / at a certain point.

  • We started off with a quick meeting.
  • He started off as an intern, now he runs the team.

send off

chunk

把东西寄出去、发出去，人交给邮政/系统之后就不在你手上了。

To post or dispatch something so it's on its way.

  • I sent off the documents yesterday.
  • Have you sent off the application yet?

set off / take off

chunk

出发、启程。set off 是人上路；take off 是飞机离地。

Set off = to start a journey. Take off = a plane leaves the ground.

  • We set off at 7am to beat the traffic.
  • The plane took off on time.

pay off

chunk

两个意思：把钱还清；付出终于有了回报。

To clear a debt in full, or for effort to finally bring a reward.

  • All the hard work finally paid off.
  • It took years, but we paid off the mortgage.

slack off

chunk

偷懒摸鱼，该干的活松了劲。

To be lazy and stop putting in effort, especially at work.

  • I've been slacking off at work all day.
  • The moment the boss leaves, everyone slacks off.

get dumped

chunk

被甩了，对方主动结束的那种。

To be broken up with by your partner.

  • He dumped me over text — so cold.
  • She got dumped a week before their anniversary.

be over being + adj/n

chunk

受够了这个状态，不想再这样下去了。

To be sick of a situation and done putting up with it.

  • I'm over being single. 受够单身了
  • She's over being treated like a child.
  • I'm over working overtime all the time.

同义： I'm done with… / I'm sick of…。后面接 being + adj/n，或 doing。

catch up on

chunk

把落下的进度补回来。

To do work you fell behind on and get current again.

  • I stayed late to catch up on work.
  • I need to catch up on sleep this weekend.

自然搭配：catch up on work / sleep / emails / the news。别说 "catch up on the progress"（不地道）。

bump into

chunk

偶遇——没约就撞见了熟人。

To meet someone by chance, without planning it.

  • I bumped into my old teacher at the supermarket.
  • Funny bumping into you here!

近义区别：run into 最万能；bump into 更口语随意；come across 偏"偶然发现某物/信息"，不只对人 —— He came across an old photo while cleaning.

pop into

chunk

顺道、快速进去一下，待不久那种；也说念头"突然冒出来"（pop into my head）。

To go into a place quickly and briefly — or for a thought to suddenly come to mind.

  • I'll just pop into the shop for some milk. 顺道进去一下
  • Can you pop into my office for a sec? 进来一下
  • A weird idea popped into my head. 突然冒出个念头

同家族：pop in 顺道来一下 · pop out 出去一会儿 · pop over / round 过来串个门。

looking back / look back on

chunk

"现在回想起来……"——常放句首，引出对过去的感慨；look back on 是回顾/回味一段经历。也可字面"回头看"。

"Looking back, …" = now that I reflect on it; look back on = to reflect on the past.

  • Looking back, I should've taken that job. 现在回想起来
  • She looks back on her uni years fondly. 回味大学时光
  • He looked back to check the door was locked. 字面：回头看

lead up to / the lead-up

chunk

leading up to 某事 = 通往、临近那件事的那段时间里（越来越近，那件事是时间终点）；也有"一步步铺垫、引向"的意思。关键：那件事只是参照点，不一定是你造成的。

In the run-up to an event (X is the reference point); or to gradually build toward it — not always cause it.

  • I had diarrhoea the whole week leading up to the wedding. 婚礼前那一整周
  • In the lead-up to the election, tensions rose. 大选前那阵子
  • the events leading up to the war 一步步导致战争的那些事（这句才带因果）

名词形式写作 the lead-up（带连字符）。diarrhoea 不可数，别加 the。

go on / where it goes

chunk

东西"套上去 / 放在……上面 / 属于哪里"。问东西该放哪也用它。

Where something is placed, fitted onto, or belongs.

  • Does this jacket go on over the hoodie?
  • Does this sauce go on the chips or the burger?
  • Where does this go? / Does this go here?

近义家族：be placed on / listed on / included on / belong on。

flip (family)

chunk

核心是"翻/突然一下"。翻书、突然发火、翻个面、拨开关、竖中指。

A quick flip or turn — of pages, of mood, of a switch.

  • I was flipping through a magazine. 随手翻
  • He flipped / flipped out when he heard the news. 突然炸了
  • On the flip side, it saves us money. 换个角度
  • Flip the switch. / He flipped me off 🖕

go through

chunk

熬过一段苦日子、经历一堆事。

To live through a hard or difficult experience.

  • He went through a lot when he first moved to Australia.
  • She's going through a rough patch right now.

go over

chunk

从头到尾仔细过一遍——检查、复习、或一起讨论。

To carefully review or run through something together.

  • Let's go over the plan one more time before the meeting.
  • Can you go over my essay and check for mistakes?

come down with

chunk

开始生病了，感觉要感冒/发烧那种苗头。

To start catching an illness, usually a mild one.

  • I think I've come down with a cold.
  • Half the office has come down with the flu.

come across as

chunk

给人一种……的印象（不一定是真的，是别人看你的感觉）。

To give people a certain impression of what you're like.

  • He comes across as arrogant, but he's actually shy.
  • Be careful — that email comes across as rude.

take over / get over

chunk

take over = 接手、接管；get over = 从（打击/病/感情）里走出来。

Take over = take control of. Get over = recover from something.

  • Can you take over for me while I'm on leave?
  • It took him months to get over the breakup.

roll over

chunk

没用完的东西自动结转到下个周期。

For something unused to carry over to the next period.

  • The unused data rolls over to next month.
  • Do my leave days roll over if I don't use them?

top up

chunk

加满、充值——把少了的补上去。

To add more to bring something back up to full.

  • Let me top up your glass.
  • I need to top up my phone / transport card.

draw lots

chunk

抽签决定。

To decide something randomly by drawing lots.

  • We couldn't decide, so we drew lots.

catch someone off guard

chunk

让人措手不及，完全没防备的时候被击中。

To surprise someone when they're completely unprepared.

  • The question really caught me off guard.
  • Her sudden resignation caught everyone off guard.

let's bounce

chunk

走吧、撤了，很随意的口语。

Casual slang for "let's leave / let's get out of here."

  • This party's dead — let's bounce.

have been + doing / adj

grammar

从过去某点一直持续到现在的状态或动作。

A state or action that started earlier and is still going now.

  • My foot has been numb since last night.
  • I have been feeling off all week.

Grammar & Usage

语法 · 辨析5 / 7

few / fewer vs little / less

grammar

能数的用 few/fewer（人、朋友、选项）；不能数的用 little/less（钱、时间、水）。

Few/fewer for countable things; little/less for uncountable.

  • Few people · fewer options left （可数）
  • Less money · less time left （不可数）

加 "a" 就是"没那么少、还有一点"：a few friends（有几个）· a little money（有点钱）。不加 a 则强调"很少"。

left vs rest（剩下）

grammar

rest = 一个整体里"其余那部分"；left = 发生了什么之后"还剩着的"。

Rest = the other part of a whole. Left = what remains after something happened.

  • I'll finish the rest of the chicken. 本来有的里剩下的
  • Two days left · three tickets left · no money left

left 常放名词后面（two days left）；rest 常用 the rest / the rest of + n。

rarely vs barely

grammar

rarely 说的是"很少发生"（频率）；barely 说的是"几乎没有 / 勉强"（程度）。

Rarely = frequency (seldom). Barely = degree (only just).

  • I rarely eat out. 很少出去吃
  • I barely passed the exam. 勉强及格

by Friday

grammar

"最晚周五之前"——截止到那个点，不能更晚。

No later than Friday — the deadline is that point.

  • Send it by Friday. / By the end of Friday at the latest.

frequency: every 2 yrs / twice

grammar

表达频率的两种说法。

Ways to state how often something happens.

  • Every 2 years 每两年一次
  • Twice every three years 三年里两次

one of the three (of them)

grammar

三个里的一个。加不加 of them 都行，意思一样。

Both forms are fine and mean the same thing.

  • One of the three is broken.
  • One of the three of them didn't show up.

past vs passed

grammar

俩词同音，写的时候老搞混。passed 只是动词 pass 的过去式；past 是"过去/经过"的名词、介词、形容词——永远不当动词。

passed = past tense of the verb "pass". past = everything else (noun / prep / adj), never a verb.

  • I passed the exam. 动词：通过
  • She passed me the salt. 动词：递
  • I walked past the shop. 介词：经过
  • Let's forget the past. 名词：过去
  • It's half past three. 介词：…点过

判断法：是"做了 pass 这个动作"（通过 / 经过 / 递 / 流逝）→ passed；其它一律 past。
